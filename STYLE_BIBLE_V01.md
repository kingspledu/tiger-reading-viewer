# Approved visual style bible

The existing Chapter 1–6 pool is the visual authority for new scenes.

- Clean, confident ink contours with softly rendered pastel backgrounds.
- Warm, readable middle-grade graphic-novel composition; expressive faces and clear gestures.
- Muted forest greens, rainy blue-gray, warm window amber, and restrained purple clothing accents.
- Use a mix of establishing, medium, over-shoulder, hand/object close-up, and reaction shots.
- Keep backgrounds recognizable but quieter than faces and foreground props.
- Preserve the existing semi-painted webtoon finish; do not switch to photorealism, anime, glossy 3D, or a different line system.
- Every generation is exactly one 4×4 grid. The grid is reviewed, then split into 16 cells.
- Existing approved images are references only. A new image cannot become a reference until reviewed.
